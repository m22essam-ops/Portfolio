Preview only Four designs for the advertising slot. All of them work: click one and pick a picture.

01 Classified

The newspaper "space to let". Quietest of the four and the only one that matches the About page's existing type without argument.

02 The real ad unit

A 300x250 medium rectangle, the exact shape everyone has been trained to ignore, with the grey nobody-bought-this ground and the little AD corner. The joke is that it is indistinguishable from the thing it is mocking.

03 Billboard

On two legs, roadside. Loudest of the four and the only one that reads as a physical object, which is a change of material on a page made of paper.

04 Ticket stub

The site's own furniture: red field, off-white type, perforated top and bottom. Belongs to the page rather than sitting on top of it, and it is the one that will still look right if the palette ever moves.
